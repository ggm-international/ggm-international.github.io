Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

To prepare the saints for the Rapture and the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ by evangelizing the lost, healing the sick, casting out demons, worshiping God alone, discipling believers, and showing love and compassion as we await His return.

Our Pastoral Leadership

Meet Our Pastoral Team

Pastor Stephen Esiobu-Chibuike & Pastor Favor Ogo Edeh-Esiobu

Under the foundational apostolic vision of our General Overseer, Bishop Dr. Paul Nwachukwu, Grace of God Mission International Atlantis is passionately led by Pastor Stephen Esiobu-Chibuike and Pastor Favor Ogo Edeh-Esiobu.

With a dynamic teaching style that blends profound biblical insights with practical everyday applications, our pastoral team empowers believers to discover their God-given purpose, walk in deliverance, and experience the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

We warmly invite you and your family to join us on this spiritual journey of faith, worship, and victory where every individual is nurtured toward spiritual excellence.

Our Core Values

What We Believe

The foundational truths that guide our faith, our community, and our mission.

We believe in one eternal God who exists as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit - Creator, Redeemer, and Sustainer of all things. (Genesis 1:1; Matthew 28:19)

The Bible is our final authority. Every decision begins with one question: “What does God’s Word say?” We study, read, and live by Scripture daily. (2 Timothy 3:16)

Every believer is a minister with a God-given purpose. We believe in the priesthood of all believers — everyone has direct access to God and a role in His mission. (1 Peter 2:9)

We practice tithing as an act of worship and trust, giving back to God a portion of what He’s given us to support His Church. (Leviticus 27:30)

We depend on the Holy Spirit’s power for daily living — to grow in character, walk in obedience, and bear spiritual fruit. (John 15:5; Ephesians 5:18)
